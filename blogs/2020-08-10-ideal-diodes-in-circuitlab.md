comment » We’re introducing a new component to the CircuitLab toolbox: the ideal diode . We’ve had semiconductor PN junction diodes since we’ve launched, which show the exponential current-voltage relationship and accurately model real-world diodes. In contrast, the ideal diode is more like a simulated on-off switch: the I-V curve would be piecewise linear.
