comment » Today we’re launching six new digital combinational gates, now available in your CircuitLab toolbox. These pair well with our recent Quick Toggle feature allowing you to rapidly do digital simulations. Four new gates are binary adders: a full adder, half adder (no carry input), 4-bit adder, and 8-bit adder: These can be used for making counters or even an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) of a small CPU.
