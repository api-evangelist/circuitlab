comment » We're excited to announce the release of a new feature that makes exploring digital circuits much easier. Instead of swapping out the digital 1 and digital 0 element in your schematic, the new Quick Toggle feature allows you to switch between a 1 and a 0 digital input by simply highlighting the element and pressing spacebar . We will likely be bringing similar functionality to other elements to help you quickly explore how changing parts of your circuit affect the behavior.
