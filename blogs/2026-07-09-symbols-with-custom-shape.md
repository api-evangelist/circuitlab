by Cadmium Hello, I am new to CircuitLab, I find it surprising that it is not possible to freely draw new symbols we create. As of now, it seems only rectangular symbols are feasible at a "custom" level. When it comes to documenting designs, this is a very limiting issue, especially when you see that standard symbols for components as basic as a simple capacitor look so odd : the plates are indeed quite elongated and with very little spacing between them.
